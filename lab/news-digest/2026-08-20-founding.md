# 2026-08-20 — Founding notes: news-digest v2.0.0

## Where this skill came from

v1.x ran privately on a Hermes agent: reading-time budget, two interest tiers,
search+RSS gathering, plain-text delivery. Two inputs produced v2.0.0:

**1. A survey of ~20 published news/briefing skills** (agents read the actual
source). Best-in-class was SeanLF/claude-rss-news-digest (state-file novelty
gating, adversarial coherence pass, delta-first headlines, transparency footer);
also notable: meridian (cross-day continuity), LeoYeAI's market-morning-brief
(fail-closed materiality gate), BankrBot's HN comment mining. Neither Anthropic's
official skills repo nor the Hermes agent repo contained any news skill.
Full credits: PROVENANCE.md.

**2. First reader triage of a live digest** (12 items). Verdicts clustered
cleanly:

- Wire-tier coverage of ongoing conflicts, rotation economics stories, and
  accident/disaster items were all rated noise — even when prominent worldwide.
- The items rated relevant were all beat-adjacent policy/technology stories.
- Stated directives: heavy-rotation stories should be monitored silently and
  surface only on a genuine breakout; ongoing conflicts belong to curated
  analysis journals, not daily wire updates ("I do not want to mimic a
  third-tier radio station"); the science section must reach beyond the
  reader's listed topics — "serendipity is the relevant thing."

## What changed in v2.0.0

1. Source-quality tiers (wires / general / science / tech / velocity / essays /
   aggregators) with tier-based dedupe.
2. Noise filters: rotation damper, curated-journal-only conflict coverage,
   accidents-are-irrelevant rule.
3. Serendipity slot, exempt from profile-fit scoring.
4. Novelty state file (`state/headlines.log`) with the "specific new fact" rule
   and delta-first headlines.
5. Coherence self-check + anti-overstatement checklist before delivery.
6. Fail-closed materiality gate and a "what was cut" footer.
7. Generic/profile split: everything personal moved out of the skill into a
   private `profile.md`.

## Open questions

- Does the rotation damper need explicit state (a "monitored stories" list), or
  is the headlines log plus judgment enough? Watch for repeat leakage.
- Serendipity quality: is one Nature/Science skim enough of a pool, or does it
  need its own gathering pass?
- Candidate next mechanisms (surveyed, not adopted): HN comment mining for tech
  items; per-source health tracking if feeds rot.

Raw triage verdicts stay private (they are taste data); these conclusions are the
public record.
